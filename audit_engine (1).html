Ideal Transaction Criteria

A transaction is classified as Ideal only if it satisfies all of the following:

  • Registered Vendor Vendor must exist in the verified registered vendors database.
  • Business Hours (7:00 AM – 7:00 PM) Transaction timestamp must fall strictly within standard operational hours.
  • Exposure Limit & Duplicacy Total amount must be $1,000 or less with zero historical duplication.
